Academic Programs & Curriculum

Surya High School offers a comprehensive secondary curriculum (Classes 6 through 10) focused on conceptual understanding, interactive sessions, and board exam preparedness.

Core Subjects Offered

Mathematics & Science

Advanced Algebra, Geometry, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology with weekly laboratory experiments.

Languages & Humanities

English (Medium of Instruction), Regional Language (Telugu/Hindi), Social Studies, History, and Geography.

Co-Curricular Skills

Computer Science & Coding Fundamentals, Fine Arts, Physical Education, and Moral Science.
