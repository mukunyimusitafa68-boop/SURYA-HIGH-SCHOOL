A Message from the Principal

"It is a privilege to address you as the Principal of Surya High School. Our school has always stood as a beacon of learning, discipline, and character, and I am truly honored to lead this vibrant community into its next chapter."

At Surya High School, we believe that education goes far beyond textbooks and examinations. Our mission is to nurture curious minds, build strong moral values, and equip every student with the confidence and skills needed to thrive in an ever-changing world. Just as the sun brings light and energy, we aim to ignite a lifelong passion for knowledge and excellence within every learner who walks through our gates."

"To our dedicated teachers and staff, thank you for your passion, resilience, and commitment to shaping young lives. To our parents and guardians, thank you for your continued trust and partnership; your support is the backbone of our students' success. And to our dear students, remember that you are capable of extraordinary things. Dream big, work hard, respect one another, and never stop asking questions."

— Dr. Rajesh Sharma, Principal

Life & Facilities at Surya High

Smart Classrooms

Bright, well-ventilated rooms equipped with smart boards and digital modules to make lessons visually engaging.

Advanced Laboratories

Dedicated Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs allowing students to test hypotheses through direct experiments.

Sports & Athletics

Expansive sports fields, basketball courts, and indoor game arenas promoting physical wellness and teamwork.

Resourceful Library

Over 10,000 reference books, journals, and quiet reading spaces to foster a deep love for independent reading.

Computer & Coding Lab

Modern computer terminals with high-speed internet where students learn fundamental programming and IT skills.

Cultural & Arts Clubs

Regular events celebrating music, drama, debate, and fine arts to build public speaking and confidence.
